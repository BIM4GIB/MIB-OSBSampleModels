OSB DIRECTOR

From flight to final take.

  1. Scout. Click Enter flight, then use WASD to move. Drag to look, or lock your mouse.
  2. Perform. Press R to record. Q/E change altitude; Shift boosts. Press B to mark a frame. R stops and opens the path editor.
  3. Refine. Select a point on the route or timeline. Drag its arrows, edit lens/aim, and change the seconds to the next point.
  4. Screen. Play or scrub the take. Compare the original recording with your edited curve. Export your take as JSON.
