DIMENSIONS AND SCALE
This is a fictional exhibition terrace, not a site plan of Fuller's projects.
Sphere: 30 bars, 60 endpoints, 90 cables. Displayed bar length 2.8 m.
Dymaxion: full-size working diameter 10.9728 m (36 ft), mast height 6.7056 m
(22 ft). Displayed at 1:2. Other dimensions are photographic estimates.
Montreal: nominal 76 m diameter and 62 m height; displayed at 1:10. The two
triangular layers and depth spacers are a simplified six-frequency study,
not a reconstruction of the original space-frame node topology.
Carbondale: approximate 39 ft 1 in diameter, 16 ft 6 in height. Displayed at
1:2. Three-frequency icosahedral cap, five blue bays. Bay clipping planes,
glazing openings and domestic fittings are estimated. No surveyed claim.

REPRESENTATION
Twelve-sided rods and 32-96-sided circular extrusions are deliberate faceting
approximations. All such members are exact bounded polygonal prisms; the
underlying profile and height remain editable. Large analytic cylinder
display exceeded the existing material triangle budget; no silent display
fallback or kernel patch is included. Roof gores are supported homothetic
section lofts. Carbondale panels and window bays are bounded extrusions,
including openings. Concealed connections and fabrication are deferred.
The Dymaxion has 48 roof sectors and 24 simplified radial floor spokes;
the interior is illustrative fit-out, not the historic measured layout.
The Montreal inner building is simplified massing.
The Montreal rods use the modeller's ordinary editable polygonal extrusion
constructor. They are checked through the complete model build, but do not
carry an analytic exact-solid document. Expanding all of them as analytic
recipes exceeded the existing 1,900,000-byte scene source/index budget.
This representation is explicit in scene.json and geometry-checks.json.

ANIMATION / VALIDATION
The sphere is independently constructed by rotating icosahedral edge struts
about their midpoint radial axes. Its unloaded nominal form admits positive
cable force densities (2.618...) and negative bar force density (-1).
The solver residual is recorded in motion-checks.json. This establishes a
self-stress equilibrium only, not stiffness, stability or load capacity.
Motion translates rigid bars radially; the cables reconnect their endpoints
and change length. Intermediate poses are not presented as force-balanced.
101 sampled poses check invariant bar length and positive non-incident
bar/bar and bar/cable clearances. No continuous collision proof is claimed.
geometry-checks.json records positive-volume, closed single solids and
representative dimension/placement edits surviving undo/redo and reopen.
Browser performance is measured in References & study notes; GPU timing is
not measured. No engineering export has been requested or certified.

NEW TRANSFORMATION STUDIES
05 Jitterbug: eight rigid equilateral triangles. Cyclic signed coordinates
(a, b, 0), (0, a, b), (b, 0, a), with a^2+b^2-a*b fixed. Cuboctahedral
start, regular icosahedral vertex arrangement at 38.1966%, and octahedral
limit. Twelve hinge positions pair into six at the final limit. Triangle
edges remain 2.33345 m. The subsequent tetrahedral fold is not included.
Six missing edges are not invented at the icosahedral position: only the
eight rigid triangles of the mechanism are shown. Displayed plate insets
and edge thicknesses are illustrative; closing edge pairs intentionally
coincide. No physical hinge/hardware clearance simulation is claimed.

06 Dymaxion map: Gray-Fuller spherical projection, Air-Ocean hinge tree.
The 20 original icosahedral faces use the 24-piece split arrangement
documented by d3-geo-polygon. Every piece has a rigid motion about its
shared hinge edge. See MAP-CREDITS.txt for projection and coastline sources.
Natural Earth 110m land is sampled at 300x300 per full face and simplified
for editable relief. Inland water and sub-resolution islands are omitted.
No image of a published BFI map is copied. The flat net has no area overlap.
The map is saved flat in the native model; the Jitterbug is saved open.

NEW-STUDY VALIDATION
201 sampled poses preserve triangle edge lengths and shared hinge endpoints
to floating-point tolerance (less than 4e-15 m in the checks).
30,096 triangle-pair checks across 99 intermediate poses found zero strict
interior crossings. Shared edges and vertices are excluded; the checks use
ideal zero-thickness facets, not a continuous physical collision proof.
Full model: 3,235 elements; 334 new editable polygonal extrusions. A separate
representative triangular extrusion passed the exact solid check, but the
new native elements do not carry individual analytic solid documents.
Placement editing survives undo/redo and serialized save/reopen.
The earlier 651 analytic exact elements remain unchanged.
