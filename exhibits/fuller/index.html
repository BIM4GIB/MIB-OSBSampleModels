Fuller / exhibition studies

Ideas made visible.

This is an architectural and geometric study, presented on a fictional exhibition terrace. Each building has its own stated scale. Dimensions and simplified details are recorded below.

The assembled viewing model preserves independently named elements. The Montreal lattice and museum, added house furnishings and two transformation studies use ordinary editable polygonal extrusions. The earlier architectural elements use exact-kernel solids. Representative new triangular solids also passed separate exact-kernel probes. Round members use faceted polygonal extrusions: 12 sides for rods, 32–96 for circular furniture and decks. The browser animation changes presentation poses; the native model stores the assembled state. The sphere’s nominal self-stress check does not establish stability or load capacity.
