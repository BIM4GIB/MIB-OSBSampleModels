ANIMATION / EDITABILITY
Mouse slider, Start/End, Play/Pause, arrow/Home/End keys, step buttons, reverse
view, inside view. Multi-stage paths 10 seconds, single-stage paths 7 seconds.
Elapsed-time timing; hidden-tab playback pauses. Nothing plays automatically.
Reduced-motion preference disables Play but retains slider control.
Motion follows prescribed reversible paths; no force, friction or wood
deformation simulation. Native modeller opens assembled geometry and authoring
history. Animation is stored in the separate presentation sidecar.
Native hidden stock/cutters remain editable and are included in its browser's
element count. Profiles and dimensions live in the generator. Native edits
require regenerating the presentation to update its static display.
