About this study

Read the cuts.

These are illustrative models of documented joint forms, with dimensions chosen for display. They are not measured historic specimens or fabrication drawings. Natural timber colour is interpreted; the fine grain is a procedural presentation material.

Study 11 is a modern Kawai-inspired construction. Its sector boundaries differ by 0.4° from the equal-sector demonstration, and a small central core belongs to one half. These explicit accommodations preserve three orientations but make the halves complementary rather than identical.

Study 12 reconstructs the three-way mitred corner demonstrated by carpenter Takami Kawai at Suikoushya. The crossed rail tongues and twin-tenoned upright are modelled as an ordered, reversible assembly. Proportions and internal cut depths are estimated, not measured from the specimen.
